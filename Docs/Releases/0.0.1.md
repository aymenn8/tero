# Tero 0.0.1

The first downloadable beta of Tero for Mac. Requires macOS 14 or later.

- Act on selected text: correct, rewrite, translate, draft replies, summarize and improve AI prompts.
- Keep useful copies in Smart Clipboard, pin entries and apply actions to older copies.
- Work with files and images: rename suggestions, conversion, resizing, OCR and PDF tools. Office conversion requires a local LibreOffice installation.
- Review Calendar events and Reminders before creating them.
- Connect your account through your browser and manage your plan on the website.
- Customize shortcuts for Tero, Clipboard and individual actions.

Tero is in beta. Selection support depends on what each application exposes to macOS accessibility.
