## What's new

- **Use Tero free, without an account.** Tero now runs AI actions with your own provider key: OpenAI, Anthropic (Claude), Grok, Google Gemini, OpenRouter, Mistral, Groq, DeepSeek, a local model with Ollama or LM Studio, or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint. Set it up in Settings → AI.
- Your key stays in your Mac's Keychain, and requests go straight from your Mac to your provider. With a local model, nothing leaves your Mac.
- Web answers, Check a claim and product suggestions need OpenAI, Anthropic or OpenRouter.
- **Download YouTube videos** as MP4 or MP3 into the folder you choose. Uses your installed yt-dlp (`brew install yt-dlp ffmpeg`).
- Tero Pro remains available for people who prefer AI included without managing keys.

## Changes

- The free daily AI allowance is replaced by your own AI key.
- The welcome tour no longer asks you to sign in.

## Improvements

- Redesigned Settings: an AI page with official provider logos, quieter shortcut controls and consistent buttons.
- Tero is now open source: github.com/aymenn8/tero
